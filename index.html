How Trackd protects your privacy

We built Trackd for people who want to find forgotten subscriptions—not for people who want to hand over their data. Here's exactly how it works.

📧 What Trackd accesses in your Gmail

  • Billing-related email metadata — sender, subject, date (for receipts, invoices, renewals)
  • Only with your explicit consent via Google's OAuth consent screen
  • Never reads personal emails, contacts, or documents
  • Never sends, deletes, or modifies any email

Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly

🏠 Where your data lives

  • Email analysis runs entirely in your browser — no data leaves your machine
  • Detected subscriptions are stored locally using chrome.storage.local
  • No cloud account required. No data uploaded to our servers.
  • You can delete all stored data by uninstalling the extension

🔑 You stay in control

  • Grant access once via Google's OAuth screen — you see exactly what's requested
  • Revoke access at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • No ongoing background sync — Trackd only scans when you ask it to
  • Uninstall the extension and all locally stored data is gone

📜 Our commitments

  • We never sell, share, or transmit your email data
  • We never request bank credentials or Plaid access
  • Full privacy policy on the Chrome Web Store listing before launch
  • Open-source options for the core detection engine under consideration

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to give Trackd access to my Gmail?
This is the most important question — here's the honest answer. Trackd requests the read-only Gmail API scope (gmail.readonly). That means we can read email metadata (sender, subject, date) for billing-related messages — but we cannot send, delete, or modify any email. The analysis runs entirely in your browser — your email data never leaves your computer. You can revoke access instantly from Google's permissions page, and uninstalling the extension deletes all locally stored data. We deliberately built Trackd this way because we believe subscription tracking shouldn't require surrendering your privacy.
What happens to my data?
It stays on your device. Subscription detection runs locally in your browser using chrome.storage.local. We never upload your email data, subscriptions, or personal information to any server. No cloud account is required to use Trackd. If you ever want to remove everything, just uninstall the extension — all data goes with it.
Is this different from Rocket Money?
Yes — fundamentally different. Rocket Money requires full bank account access via Plaid, giving it read access to every transaction in your bank accounts. Trackd only needs read-only Gmail access to billing emails. We can’t see your bank balance, transactions, or anything outside billing-related emails. And Rocket Money is US-only — Trackd works globally wherever Gmail does.
What does “read-only Gmail access” mean exactly?
It means Trackd can view email metadata (sender, subject, date) and content for billing-related messages only. We request the gmail.readonly OAuth scope from Google, which explicitly prevents us from sending, deleting, or modifying any email. We filter for receipts, invoices, renewal notices, and free trial confirmations. Personal emails are never read or processed. You can see exactly which permissions you're granting on Google's consent screen before you approve, and you can revoke them at any time.
How much will it cost?
A free tier will cover most people (up to 10 subscriptions, monthly scan). A pro tier (unlimited subs, weekly scans, cancellation guides) will be around $3-4/month. Early waitlist members will get a discount at launch.
Does this work outside the US?
Yes. As long as you use Gmail, Trackd works anywhere in the world and supports multiple currencies. Rocket Money is US-only—this is one of the main reasons we’re building Trackd.
