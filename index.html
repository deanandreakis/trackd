Frequently asked questions

Is this different from Rocket Money?
Yes. Rocket Money requires full bank account access via Plaid. Trackd only needs read-only Gmail access. We can’t see your bank balance, transactions, or anything outside your email receipts. Also, Rocket Money is US-only—Trackd works globally.
What does “read-only Gmail access” mean?
We request the Gmail API scope that lets us read email metadata and content only for billing-related messages (receipts, invoices, subscription confirmations). We never read personal emails, and you can revoke access at any time from your Google account settings.
What happens to my data?
We process as much as possible locally in your browser. Subscription data is stored on your device. We never sell your data. We never share your data. Our privacy policy will spell this out in more detail before launch.
How much will it cost?
A free tier will cover most people (up to 10 subscriptions, monthly scan). A pro tier (unlimited subs, weekly scans, cancellation guides) will be around $3-4/month. Early waitlist members will get a discount at launch.
Does this work outside the US?
Yes. As long as you use Gmail, Trackd works anywhere in the world and supports multiple currencies. Rocket Money is US-only—this is one of the main reasons we’re building Trackd.
