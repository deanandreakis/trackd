You can revoke access at any time. If you join the beta testing group, we keep your email address for that purpose only, and you can ask us to delete it at any time. This policy spells out the details below.

2. What the extension accesses

Trackd requests read-only access to your Gmail account via Google's OAuth 2.0 API. Specifically, we request the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly scope. This allows Trackd to:

  • Read email metadata (sender, subject line, date) for billing-related messages
  • Read email content for messages that appear to be receipts, invoices, renewal notices, free trial confirmations, or subscription-related communications

Trackd does not request or have the ability to:

  • Send, delete, or modify any email
  • Access your contacts, calendar, or Google Drive
  • Read personal or non-billing emails (we filter for billing-related patterns only)
  • Access your bank accounts, payment credentials, or financial accounts

3. How your data is processed

All email analysis and subscription detection runs entirely in your browser using client-side JavaScript:

  1. Trackd fetches a list of recent billing-related emails from Gmail using the read-only API.
  2. The extension analyses sender names, subject lines, and email patterns locally in your browser to detect known subscription merchants.
  3. Detected subscriptions are stored using chrome.storage.local — browser-local storage that stays on your device.
  4. Your email content is never transmitted to any external server, including ours.

4. What the beta signup collects (website)

When you submit the form on trackd.deanware.com "Join the testing group":

  • The email address you type in. We use it to (a) add you to the closed testing group, (b) email you install/download instructions, and (c) contact you about the testing group itself. If — and only if — you tick the optional marketing box, we also use it to send occasional Trackd product updates.
  • Submission metadata added by our form host (Netlify): your IP address, browser user-agent, and the submission timestamp. We do not use this for profiling; it exists so that spam and abuse can be filtered and investigated.
  • What we do NOT collect on the website: no name, no phone number, no payment data, no Gmail data, no account password. There is no Trackd account system.

We do not use the beta email list for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone except the processors named in section 7.

The install-instructions email itself is sent on our behalf through our email provider (Resend) from our Netlify form handler. Your address is passed to that provider only so the message can be delivered; it is not added to any third-party mailing list.

5. Analytics

We use a self-hosted Umami analytics instance (umami.andreakis.org) to collect anonymous usage statistics about the landing page. Umami collects:

  • Page views and referrer URLs
  • Browser type and device category
  • Country-level location (approximate, IP-based)

Umami does not use cookies and does not build a profile of you; the data is aggregated statistics.

The Chrome extension also sends anonymised usage events (for example, that a scan ran or that a subscription was confirmed) to the same self-hosted Umami instance. These events contain only usage statistics (such as the event type and a randomly generated session identifier) and never include email content, message metadata, subscription data, or any personally identifiable information.

Cookies: the website sets no cookies of its own, and neither Umami nor the beta signup form requires a cookie. We therefore show no cookie banner. If we ever add a cookie-based service, we will add a consent banner first.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights. To exercise any of them, email trackd@deanware.com. We will respond within 30 days and we will not charge you or degrade your access for asking.

  • Access — get a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — correct an inaccurate email address.
  • Erasure — have your data deleted (section 6).
  • Restriction / objection — ask us to stop or limit processing; for marketing, an objection is always honoured.
  • Portability — receive your data in a common machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw the signup or marketing consent at any time; withdrawal does not affect processing done before it.
  • Non-discrimination (CCPA/CPRA) — we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.
  • Complain — lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (EU/UK) or the California Attorney General / CPPA. We would appreciate the chance to fix it first.

California notice (CCPA/CPRA): in the last 12 months we have collected the categories "identifiers" (email address, IP address) and "internet or network activity" (analytics, user-agent). Purposes are as described above. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, so there is no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link because there is nothing to opt out of. We do not knowingly collect data from consumers under 16.

Do Not Track / Global Privacy Control: we do not track you across other websites, so there is nothing to turn off; GPC signals have no effect because we do not sell or share personal information.

9. Legal bases (GDPR, for EEA/UK users)

  • Beta signup and install-instruction emails — your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) and/or performance of the service you requested (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Optional product-update emails — your consent only (Art. 6(1)(a)), given with a separate, unticked checkbox.
  • Spam filtering / abuse prevention — our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) in keeping the form usable and secure.
  • Aggregated, cookieless analytics — our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) in measuring whether our promotion works, using data that does not identify you.
  • Gmail analysis inside the extension — your consent, given through the Google OAuth screen; revocable at any time.

10. Security

  • Least privilege by design: one read-only Google scope, no other permission.
  • On-device processing: email analysis and merchant detection run entirely in your browser; no message content is transmitted to or stored on any external server.
  • Local-only storage: detected subscriptions live in chrome.storage.local and are removed when you uninstall.
  • Encryption in transit: HTTPS/TLS for the Gmail API, the signup form, and Gumroad licence verification.
  • No backend, no account, no cloud database for extension data.
  • Segregated contact data: the beta list lives in the form host's submission store, not in any product database.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we collect as little as possible so that there is as little as possible to protect.

11. Children

Trackd is not directed to children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA/UK), and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has submitted an email address to us, contact us and we will delete it.
